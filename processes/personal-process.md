# Personal Process

**Content type:** Selectable profile requirements and guidance

## Applicability

These requirements apply when a project selects the WSP personal-process
profile. They are informed by the Personal Software Process (PSP) framework.

## Requirements

### WSP-PSP-0001 — Defined Personal Process

Each in-scope engineer shall follow a documented personal work process that
identifies planning, design, implementation, review, verification, and
postmortem activities applicable to the assigned work.

**PSP relationship:** Defined process and process scripts.

**Verification:** Personal-process and work-record inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0002 — Work Estimate

Before implementation, each in-scope work item shall have an estimate of size,
effort, and completion date at a level proportional to the work.

The estimate shall use relevant historical data when sufficient comparable data
exists. Assumptions and uncertainty shall be recorded.

**PSP relationship:** Size, resource, and schedule estimation.

**Verification:** Estimate and planning-record inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0003 — Time Recording

Actual effort shall be recorded by defined work phase with enough accuracy to
compare planned and actual effort and improve later estimates.

Interruptions and excluded time shall be handled consistently by the project's
measurement definition.

**PSP relationship:** Time recording log.

**Verification:** Time record and measurement-definition inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0004 — Defect Recording

Each defect found in in-scope work shall record, where known:

- defect type;
- phase injected;
- phase removed;
- time required to diagnose and correct it; and
- related requirement, review, test, or change.

The project shall define a consistent defect classification scheme.

**PSP relationship:** Defect recording log and defect-type standard.

**Verification:** Defect-record and classification inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0005 — Personal Reviews

In-scope designs and source changes shall receive a personal review before
formal verification when such a review can remove defects earlier.

The engineer shall use a checklist informed by personal or relevant project
defect history. Review findings shall feed the defect record without requiring
the disclosure of protected personal measures.

**PSP relationship:** Design review, code review, and review checklists.

**Verification:** Review record, checklist, and defect-trace inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0006 — Quality Plan

The work plan shall identify the review and verification activities expected to
prevent or remove defects before release.

High-risk work shall allocate explicit effort for requirements review, design
review, code review, static analysis, and testing as applicable.

**PSP relationship:** Quality planning and early defect removal.

**Verification:** Work plan and completed-quality-activity inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0007 — Postmortem

At completion of an in-scope work item or defined reporting period, the engineer
shall compare planned and actual size, effort, schedule, defects, and quality
activities.

The postmortem shall identify material estimation errors, recurring defect
patterns, and candidate process improvements.

**PSP relationship:** Postmortem process and process-improvement proposals.

**Verification:** Postmortem-record inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0008 — Personal Process Improvement

Personal estimates, checklists, and process steps shall be updated when repeated
evidence demonstrates that a change is likely to improve planning accuracy,
early defect removal, or product quality.

A personal-process change shall not alter a controlled project process without
normal project review and approval.

**PSP relationship:** Process improvement based on measured performance.

**Verification:** Process-history and improvement-decision inspection.

### WSP-PSP-0009 — Personal Data Protection

The project shall define access, retention, aggregation, and approved uses for
personal time, estimate, and defect data.

Personal measures shall not be used as standalone performance rankings or
compared across engineers without context, consent, and approved governance.

**PSP relationship:** WSP governance extension for responsible use of personal
process measurements.

**Verification:** Data-governance policy and access inspection.

# Template — REQ-NNNN: Short Requirement Title

**Content type:** Template

**Status:** Proposed

**Source:** Stakeholder, standard, higher-level requirement, ADR, or issue

## Scope

Identify the systems, configurations, operating conditions, and lifecycle
stages to which this requirement applies. State important exclusions.

## Requirement

The responsible system, component, or process shall perform an observable
behavior or satisfy a measurable constraint under stated conditions.

Separate independently verifiable obligations into distinct requirements.

## Rationale

Explain why the requirement exists. Do not introduce additional obligations in
the rationale.

## Verification

**Method:** Test, inspection, analysis, review, or demonstration

**References:** `TC-NNNN` or another controlled verification artifact

Describe the evidence and pass criteria when the reference alone is
insufficient.

## Relationships

- **Derived from:** Higher-level requirement or source
- **Depends on:** Related requirement or architectural constraint
- **Conflicts with:** Known competing requirement, if applicable

## Tailoring

Describe permitted applicability conditions or tailoring constraints. Write
`None` when tailoring beyond the normal WSP adoption process is not defined.

## Implementation Record

Reference the current design or implementation that satisfies the requirement.
This section is informative and may change without altering the requirement.

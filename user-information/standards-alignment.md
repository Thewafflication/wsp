# ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022 Alignment

**Content type:** Standards guidance

## Position

ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022, *Systems and software engineering — Design and
development of information for users*, is the principal reference for this
profile. Its published scope covers determining what information software
users need, choosing how it is presented, developing it, making it available,
and maintaining its design throughout the life cycle. It also addresses the
structure, content, and format of information for users, including information
incorporated into a user interface.

WSP translates those concerns into original, implementation-oriented
requirements. It does not reproduce the standard.

```text
Audience, tasks, needs, constraints, and information plan
        ↓ determine
Information architecture, content types, media, and acceptance criteria
        ↓ developed and controlled through
Drafting, review, validation, accessibility, and configuration management
        ↓ synchronized through
Packaging, release, feedback, updating, and maintenance
```

## Referenced Edition and Public Structure

The ISO catalogue identifies ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022 as edition 1, published in
January 2022. The official public table of contents exposes the following
principal areas used by this profile:

- Clause 5 — information management, including project planning and management;
- Clause 6 — project requirements and constraints, audience and task analysis,
  development, reviews, prototypes and drafts, content management, translation,
  final assembly, packaging, release, maintenance, and change control; and
- Clause 7 — information quality, including correctness.

The standard normatively references IEC/IEEE 82079-1:2019. WSP does not claim
coverage or conformity to that separate standard merely through adoption of
this profile.

## Conformity Position

Adopting this profile does not establish conformity with ISO/IEC/IEEE
26514:2022. A project making a contractual or conformity claim needs authorized
access to the complete standard, a controlled clause-level applicability and
evidence matrix, resolved gaps and tailoring, and any required independent
assessment. Public catalogue material is sufficient to explain WSP's thematic
alignment, but not to make a conformity determination.

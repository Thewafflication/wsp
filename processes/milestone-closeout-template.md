# Template — Milestone Closeout Record

**Content type:** Template

**Milestone or work package:** Identifier and title

**Status:** Proposed / Complete / Incomplete / Superseded

**Completion date:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Inherited baseline:** Starting source revision or predecessor record

**Completed baseline:** Final source revision and controlled artifact set

**Tested baseline:** Tested source, binary, configuration, and evidence identity

**Owner:** Responsible person or role

**Approval:** Controlled approval reference

## Outcome

Summarize the observable result, delivered scope, and principal limitations.
Do not equate implementation completion with verified completion.

## Scope Accounting

| Planned item | Final disposition | Evidence or later allocation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deliverable or objective | Complete / Deferred / Removed / Superseded | Artifact, approval, or later milestone |

## Exit Criteria

| Criterion | Evidence | Gate | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Planned completion condition | Test, report, review, or artifact | Required / Informative | Pass / Fail / N/A |

Every required exit gate shall pass before the work package is reported
complete. `N/A` requires an approved applicability rationale.

## Verification Summary

| Configuration or method | Build or preparation | Verification | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform, architecture, build, inspection, or analysis | Pass / Fail / N/A | Result summary | Complete / Incomplete |

Reference the authoritative test report and retained evidence rather than
duplicating detailed execution output.

## Review and Finding Summary

| Review or finding | Disposition | Evidence, approval, or condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review record or finding ID | Resolved / Deferred / Accepted risk / Open | Reference |

## Completed Artifacts and Baselines

| Artifact | Revision or digest | Purpose | Retention location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source, binary, document, evidence, or package | Immutable identity | Delivered use | Location |

## Deferred Objectives and Accepted Risk

| Item | Impact | Owner | Target milestone or release | Compensating control | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Objective, finding, or risk | Consequence | Role | Identifier or condition | Control or `None` | Reference |

Approved deferred objectives do not by themselves prevent closeout or product
release. They remain outside completion and verification claims and shall not
also be required gates for the completed work package or current release.

## Estimate and Measurement Review

This section is optional unless selected requirements make measurement
mandatory. Record unavailable measures as unavailable; do not reconstruct
precision the available evidence cannot support.

| Measure | Planned | Actual | Variance and interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Size, effort, schedule, tests, defects, or review findings | Value | Value | Difference and cause |

## Lessons and Process Improvement

- Estimation, review, defect, verification, or workflow lesson
- Approved checklist, process, template, or tooling change

## Handoff and Release Effect

Identify downstream dependencies, retained compatibility or security
constraints, required support action, documentation changes, and whether the
completed baseline is intended for a product release.

## Closeout Decision

- **Decision:** Complete / Do not complete / Complete with documented deferrals
- **Approver:** Person or role
- **Required gates:** All Pass / Not satisfied
- **Rationale:** Evidence-backed decision basis
- **Next review or release action:** Reference or `None`

## References

- Accepted plan, design record, review, test report, evidence inventory,
  approval, or successor work package
